Tidal current prediction involves forecasting the speed and direction of water flow based on the predictable movements of the earth, moon, and sun. These predictions are crucial for navigation, fishing, and marine operations. While astronomical factors are predictable, actual currents can be influenced by weather conditions like wind and storm surge, causing deviations from the predicted currents.

Tide prediction involves forecasting water levels based on the predictable movements of the earth, moon, and sun. These predictions are crucial for various activities, especially those related to navigation, water sports, and coastal management. While astronomical factors are predictable, actual water levels can be influenced by weather conditions like wind and atmospheric pressure, causing deviations from the predicted tides.
